# Sandwich Construction

## Assumptions
- We have an unobstructed countertop
- On the counter we have: 
    - a plate with two slices of bread side-by-side
    - a jar of pb
    - jar of jelly
    - a butter knife
    - napkin
    - the robot is charged and ready to go
<br><br>

## Steps to build the sandwich
1. Open the PB
2. Open the jelly
3. Pick up the knife
4. Use the knife to scoop PB from jar
5. Spread the PB on the right slice of the bread
6. Use napkin to clean the knife
7. Use the knife to scoop jelly from jar
8. Spread the jelly on the left slice of the bread
9. Use napkin to clean the knife
10. Discard napkin
11. Lay down the knife
12. Stack the bread with PB and Jelly sides touching
13. Replace lids on PB & Jelly
14. Put PB, Jelly, and Knife away

<br><br>

## Exit Conditions
- The plate and constructed sandwich are the only items on the counter
